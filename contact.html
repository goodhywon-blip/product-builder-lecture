How to reach us

Operator contact

Hangul Name Studio is operated as an independent web product. For all inquiries, email goodhywon@gmail.com.

We typically review support mail in batches and prioritize bug reports and data/privacy requests.

What to include in your message

Faster support
  • Type of request: bug report, spelling correction, privacy, or general question
  • The English name you tested and the Hangul result you received
  • Your preferred spelling and why it may read better
  • Device and browser details if you found a UI issue

Community corrections

Best route for dictionary feedback

For spelling improvements that could help all users, please submit through the Community suggestions panel on the homepage. Email is still useful for context-heavy cases or follow-up.

Support scope

What we can help with
  • Conversion behavior that appears inconsistent or unclear
  • Worksheet, card-download, or sharing feature problems
  • Privacy or data-handling questions related to this site
  • Abuse reports for harmful or low-quality community suggestions
