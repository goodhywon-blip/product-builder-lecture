Reference notes for picking readable Hangul spellings.

How to evaluate your result

Quick checklist
  • Say your name out loud and compare that sound to the Hangul result.
  • Check whether the syllable rhythm feels natural when read by Korean speakers.
  • Review alternatives if your name contains ambiguous sounds (such as th, r/l, or final consonants).
  • Choose readability over strict letter-by-letter substitution.

Common pronunciation patterns

Guidance, not strict rules

Long vowels

English long vowels may become diphthong-like Hangul forms (for example, long A often sounding like “에이”).

Consonant clusters

Clusters such as “str” or “ck” are often simplified for natural Hangul pronunciation flow.

Final sounds

Word-final consonants may shift depending on readability and typical Korean ending patterns.
